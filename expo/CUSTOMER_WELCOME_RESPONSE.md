## What is DreamBig Business OS?

DreamBig Business OS is your all-in-one business management platform designed to help you turn your business knowledge into daily action. It's like having a complete business office in your pocket!

## Getting Started (3 Simple Steps)

### Step 1: Complete Your Business Profile
- Go to the **Businesses** tab
- Fill in your business details (name, type, location, capital)
- This unlocks all features and personalizes your experience

### Step 2: Select Your DreamBig Book
- Go to **Settings** → **Business Profile**
- Choose which DreamBig book you own (e.g., "Start Your Business", "Grow Your Business", etc.)
- This unlocks relevant tools and features based on your book

### Step 3: Start Using the Tools
Once your profile is set up, you'll have access to:

**📊 Dashboard** - See your sales, expenses, and profit at a glance
**💰 Transactions** - Track all your income and expenses
**📄 Documents** - Create professional invoices, receipts, and quotations
**🛒 POS System** - Complete point-of-sale for retail businesses
**📦 Products** - Manage your inventory and products
**👥 Customers** - Keep track of your customer database
**📈 Budgets** - Plan and track your cashflow

## Quick Tips

✅ **Start Simple**: Begin by adding a few transactions or creating your first invoice
✅ **Explore Gradually**: Features unlock based on your selected book, so you won't feel overwhelmed
✅ **Use Folders**: Organize your documents into folders (e.g., "Order List", "Invoices 2025")
✅ **Multi-Currency**: The app supports both USD and ZWL with automatic exchange rate tracking

## Need Help?

- **In-App Help**: Tap the "?" icon in any screen for context-specific help
- **Support**: Feel free to reply to this email with any questions
- **Tutorials**: Check the Settings tab for video tutorials and guides

## What Makes Us Different?

Unlike generic business apps, DreamBig Business OS is **built around the books you already own**. The tools you see are directly connected to the DreamBig book you select, making it easier to apply what you've learned.
